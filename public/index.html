How to play Willowvale

1. Move aroundHold the arrow pad on your phone, or use WASD / arrow keys on a computer.
2. Find something to doTap a building or animal. If it is hard to tap, walk closer and press Interact.
3. Explore the cityUse Homes for rentals and purchases, Places for landmarks, Social for neighbours and Activities for things to try.
4. Save your progressPlay as a guest to try the controls. Create an account to use the online account features.
